    The Ritual

    Born and raised in Hermosillo, Sonora — a city where baseball runs deep. I set out to visit every MLB ballpark, always wearing my Naranjeros de Hermosillo jersey for the official photo.

    At every new stadium, I arrive early to take the traditional picture, then tour the park and soak in its landmarks. I stay for the whole game, and never leave without my souvenir soda — collecting the cup is part of the ritual.
